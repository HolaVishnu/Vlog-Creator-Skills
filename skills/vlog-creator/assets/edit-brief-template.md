# Edit Brief

## Assumptions

Record the confirmed source files, creator intent, intended audience, target platform, target duration, available editor, and any decisions that still need approval. Note any limitation that changes the recommendation, such as missing coverage, unknown music rights, or a mixed-orientation shoot.

## Creative direction

State the viewer promise, emotional tone, primary style profile, and the one impression the finished vlog should leave. Explain which visual and audio choices support that direction and which treatments are intentionally restrained.

## Story structure

Describe the hook, setup, key turning points, payoff, and closing beat. Identify the question that keeps the viewer watching and the moment that answers it.

## Footage inventory

List each stable clip ID with its path, selected time range, technical concern, semantic note, and category such as KEEP, B-ROLL, STORY, or CUT. Record missing shots and the safest workaround.

## Timeline

Write a timecoded sequence of clips, dialogue, and major transitions. For every segment, state its story purpose and the intended pace.

## Cuts and B-roll

Identify trims that remove repetition, B-roll that clarifies action or location, and any natural transition moments. Keep only coverage with a defined job in the story.

## Audio

Record dialogue cleanup decisions, ambience and room tone needs, silence beats worth preserving, approved music cues, sound effects, and mix priorities. Include licensing status for every third-party asset.

## Graphics and captions

Specify captions, names, locations, comparisons, title cards, and graphics that clarify information. Record timing, placement, contrast, and accessibility considerations.

## Color

Describe correction needed for exposure, white balance, and matching, followed by the restrained overall grade. Note skin-tone and continuity checks.

## Retention pass

List points where a first-time viewer may lose the story, then record the smallest change that restores curiosity, clarity, emotion, or pace.

## Software steps

Give the editor-neutral action followed by verified steps for the selected editor and version. State the expected result and a simple alternative if a tool is unavailable.

## Delivery

Record target resolution, frame rate, codec or platform preset, captions format, filenames, output location, and any fallback package contents. Include title, thumbnail, chapters, or upload materials only as preparation for user review.

## Final quality control

Confirm that the output exists, has readable video and audio, matches duration and format goals, and has been reviewed at the start, middle, and end. Note unresolved issues and the next exact action; publishing requires explicit user authorization.
