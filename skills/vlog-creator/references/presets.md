# Platform And Style Presets

Read this when choosing a target format, adapting a vlog, or using a named preset.

## Platform Presets

| Preset | Canvas | Default frame rate | Pacing | Packaging |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube long-form | 16:9, 1920x1080 or source-native 4K | Match source, usually 24/25/30 | Story-led with contrast; preserve complete performances when requested | Title, description, thumbnail, chapters, captions, tags, hashtags |
| Shorts/Reels/TikTok | 9:16, 1080x1920 | Match source, often 30/60 | Immediate hook, dense but clear | Safe-zone captions, no buried payoff |

Use source-native frame rate unless a platform or editor requirement says otherwise. Avoid converting 24 fps cinematic footage to 60 fps without a reason.

## Style Presets

### Travel Vlog

Viewer promise: bring the audience somewhere specific and make the payoff tangible. Open with the most vivid place, route question, obstacle, or result. Use establishing shots, details, natural ambience, map/route labels, and motivated music. Let beautiful shots breathe only when they reveal place or emotion.

For travel plus live event footage, decide whether the main promise is the journey, the performance, or both. If the performance is the main value, structure the travel/context material as a short setup and let the full songs or key performances carry the runtime.

### Concert Or Full-Show Vlog

Viewer promise: let fans relive the room, the crowd, and the performance. Preserve complete songs or complete emotional moments when the user asks for a longer watch. Remove dead pre-roll, accidental pockets/ceilings/floors, severe shake, duplicate starts/stops, and unusable audio. Add a short cinematic intro, clean title card, chapter cards between major sections, restrained transitions, and a closing card. Prioritize sync, readable orientation, stable 1080p framing, and natural concert audio over heavy effects.

### Talking-Head Vlog

Viewer promise: a clear thought, story, opinion, review, or update. Prioritize intelligible dialogue, purposeful jump cuts, B-roll covers for trims, subtle reframes, lower thirds for names/facts, and captions. Remove repetition while preserving human reactions.

### Cinematic Vlog

Viewer promise: a mood-driven story with visual care. Use measured pacing, intentional silence, natural sound, stable compositions, restrained transitions, exposure/white balance matching, and a light grade. Keep story clarity above pretty shots.

### Faceless Vlog

Viewer promise: narration and visuals carry the story without relying on an on-camera host. Use voiceover, screen recordings, hands, objects, location details, captions, labels, and stills. Confirm stock/media rights. Text and sound design may carry more structure, but should remain readable and purposeful.

### Beginner Default

If the user does not specify a target, choose YouTube long-form for horizontal footage longer than roughly two minutes, Shorts/Reels for vertical footage or short highlight reels, and ask for only the missing decision that materially changes the edit.
